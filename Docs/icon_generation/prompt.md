# Prompt for the Cooking app icon

Attach these images in this order: 1_nori_character.png, 2_pizza.png, 3_waving_hand_pose.png, 4_layout_guide.png

## Prompt (paste this)

Create a square mobile game app icon, 1024x1024, full-bleed artwork.

Image 1 is Nori, the raccoon chef character. Keep her design exactly the same: same face, grey and cream fur, dark raccoon mask markings, big brown eyes, white chef hat with yellow goggles on it, red neckerchief, white chef jacket and teal apron with a wooden spoon and whisk. Do not redesign her.
Image 2 is the pizza she holds: a tomato and basil pizza on a light blue plate. Keep it the same.
Image 3 shows how her raised open hand looks when she waves.
Image 4 is only a rough layout guide: head in the upper middle, waving hand on the right, pizza plate held in front at the bottom left.

Nori is happy with an open-mouth smile, looking at the viewer. She holds the pizza plate in front of her with one hand and waves hello with her other raised open hand. Her head, her waving hand and the pizza are all clearly visible, centered, with a comfortable margin from the edges.

Background: a smooth bright gradient from sunny yellow in the centre to soft orange at the edges, with a few tiny white four-point sparkles. No text, no letters, no border, no frame, no rounded corners, no drop shadow outside the artwork.

Style: glossy, soft 3D cartoon render matching the reference images, clean, bright and readable at very small sizes.

## Output requirements (Apple)

- 1024 x 1024 pixels, PNG
- No transparency (solid background all the way to the edges)
- Square corners (iOS rounds them itself)
- No text

## If the first result is off

- Nori looks different: add "Match image 1 exactly; only change her pose and expression."
- Pizza looks different: add "The pizza must match image 2 exactly."
- Edges or corners look rounded: add "Full-bleed square artwork, the background must fill all four corners."
